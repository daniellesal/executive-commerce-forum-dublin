The premise

Not a sales event. A forum for better conversations.

This gathering is designed to create space for Enterprise leaders to connect with peers, partners, and Shopify teams in a setting that feels useful, candid, and human.

The focus is on market presence in EMEA, strategic alignment, open dialogue, and gaining deeper insight into the priorities and challenges shaping the Enterprise commerce ecosystem.
